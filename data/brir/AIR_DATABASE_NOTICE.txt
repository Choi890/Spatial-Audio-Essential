Measured binaural room responses included by this application:
- AIR Meeting Room
- AIR Lecture Room
- AIR Stairway, frontal 90-degree orientation
- AIR Aula Carolina, frontal 3 m configuration

Each stereo WAV combines the corresponding left/right dummy-head MAT
measurements. The response before 45 ms is removed because the application
renders direct sound and directional early reflections separately. A short
fade is applied at the retained response boundaries and channel energy is
normalized for bounded browser convolution. No measured tail is repeated,
stretched, or replaced with an algorithmic reverberator.

AIR Database reference:
M. Jeub, M. Schaefer, and P. Vary,
"A Binaural Room Impulse Response Database for the Evaluation of
Dereverberation Algorithms," Proc. DSP, 2009.
