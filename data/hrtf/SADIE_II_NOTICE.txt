The original SOFA files are converted to a compact lossless Float32 runtime
container. Source positions are converted from SOFA left-positive azimuth to
the application's right-positive renderer coordinates. A single bounded
global level scale is applied per profile; directional and interaural cues are
otherwise preserved.

Citation:
C. Armstrong, L. Thresh, D. Murphy, and G. Kearney,
"A Perceptual Evaluation of Individual and Non-Individual HRTFs:
A Case Study of the SADIE II Database," Applied Sciences 8(11), 2029, 2018.
https://doi.org/10.3390/app8112029

Checksums and the deterministic conversion procedure are recorded in
tools/build_measured_hrtf_library.py.
